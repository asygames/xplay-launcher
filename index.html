Account security

Microsoft sign-in for Minecraft

Authentication on Microsoft's website.

XPlay's Minecraft module supports Microsoft's device-code flow. Players sign in on a Microsoft-hosted page instead of entering their Microsoft passwords in XPlay. Authorized Microsoft, Xbox and Minecraft services are used for account and game-access verification. XPlay does not bypass game ownership requirements.
